## Idea 1: "Institutional Intelligence" (Selected)

**Design Movement**: Institutional/Financial Tech (FinTech) Minimalism. Inspired by Bloomberg Terminal, BlackRock Aladdin, and KKR.

**Core Principles**:
1.  **Data Density & Clarity**: Information is presented with high density but extreme clarity. No fluff.
2.  **Trust & Authority**: The interface must exude confidence and reliability.
3.  **Operational Efficiency**: Workflows are direct and unobstructed.
4.  **Dark Mode First**: Designed primarily for low-light, high-focus environments.

**Color Philosophy**:
*   **Backgrounds**: Deep Slate/Black (`#0f172a`, `#020617`) to reduce eye strain and project seriousness.
*   **Text**: High contrast White (`#f8fafc`) for readability.
*   **Accents**: Subtle, professional Blues (`#3b82f6`, `#1d4ed8`) representing trust and intelligence.
*   **Status Indicators**: Muted greens and ambers, avoiding neon or overly bright warning colors.

**Layout Paradigm**:
*   **Dashboard-Centric**: A persistent sidebar for navigation and task history.
*   **Split-Screen Analysis**: Input on the left, real-time processing/results on the right.
*   **Card-Based Modules**: Distinct content areas for different agents (Sparring, Debate, Research).

**Signature Elements**:
*   **Monospace Data Points**: IDs, timestamps, and metrics in a clean monospace font.
*   **Thin Borders**: 1px subtle borders to define structure without visual weight.
*   **Status Pills**: Small, pill-shaped indicators for agent states (e.g., "Sparring", "Debating").

**Interaction Philosophy**:
*   **Instant Feedback**: Every action has an immediate, subtle visual response.
*   **Keyboard Accessibility**: Power users can navigate without a mouse.

**Animation**:
*   **Micro-interactions**: fast (150ms), precise transitions. No bouncy or springy physics.
*   **Loading States**: Skeleton screens and subtle pulse effects, not spinning loaders.

**Typography System**:
*   **Headings**: Inter (Bold/Semi-Bold) for clear hierarchy.
*   **Body**: Inter (Regular) for high readability.
*   **Data/Code**: JetBrains Mono or Roboto Mono for technical details.

## Idea 2: "Futuristic Research Lab"

**Design Movement**: Sci-Fi UI / HUD (Heads-Up Display). Inspired by Iron Man's JARVIS or Minority Report.

**Core Principles**:
1.  **Transparency & Layering**: Use of glassmorphism and semi-transparent layers.
2.  **Holographic aesthetic**: Glowing elements and neon accents.
3.  **Dynamic Data Visualization**: Real-time graphs and connecting lines.

**Color Philosophy**:
*   **Backgrounds**: Deep Navy or Void Black.
*   **Accents**: Neon Cyan and Electric Purple.

**Layout Paradigm**:
*   **Floating Modules**: Content areas that appear to float in 3D space.
*   **Central Focus**: The active agent takes center stage.

**Signature Elements**:
*   **Glowing Borders**: Elements have a faint outer glow.
*   **Tech Lines**: Decorative lines connecting related data points.

**Interaction Philosophy**:
*   **Fluidity**: Everything flows and morphs.

**Animation**:
*   **Complex Transitions**: Elements unfold and assemble.

**Typography System**:
*   **Headings**: Orbitron or similar sci-fi font.
*   **Body**: Rajdhani.

## Idea 3: "Academic Paper"

**Design Movement**: Swiss Style / International Typographic Style. Inspired by academic journals and Notion.

**Core Principles**:
1.  **Content First**: The text is the hero. UI is invisible.
2.  **Grid Precision**: Strict adherence to a grid system.
3.  **Serif Elegance**: Use of serif fonts for authority.

**Color Philosophy**:
*   **Backgrounds**: Off-white / Cream (`#fdfbf7`).
*   **Text**: Dark Charcoal (`#333`).
*   **Accents**: Brick Red or Navy Blue.

**Layout Paradigm**:
*   **Single Column**: Focus on reading flow.
*   **Marginalia**: Notes and metadata in the margins.

**Signature Elements**:
*   **Heavy Dividers**: Thick black lines separating sections.
*   **Large Typography**: Oversized headings.

**Interaction Philosophy**:
*   **Static & Stable**: Minimal movement.

**Animation**:
*   **None**: Or very subtle fades.

**Typography System**:
*   **Headings**: Merriweather or Playfair Display.
*   **Body**: Source Serif Pro.
